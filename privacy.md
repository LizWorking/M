# Privacy Policy - M-Scripts

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what data M-Scripts ("the Bot") collects and how it is used.

## 1. Data we collect
Depending on the features used, the Bot may store:
- Discord user IDs, server (guild) IDs, and channel/role IDs.
- Usernames, to display them in commands or logs.
- Settings and configuration chosen by server administrators.

We do not collect passwords, payment information, or any data outside Discord.

## 2. How we use data
Data is used only to operate the Bot's features, to keep server settings, and to prevent abuse. We do not sell or share data with third parties.

## 3. Message content
The Bot reads message content only to detect and respond to commands. Message content is not stored.

## 4. Storage and retention
Data is stored on a private server. It is kept while the Bot is in your server. When the Bot is removed, server data is deleted within 30 days.

## 5. Your rights
You can ask to see or delete your data at any time by contacting us. We will respond within a reasonable time.

## 6. Security
We take reasonable measures to protect stored data, but no system is completely secure.

## 7. Children
The Bot follows Discord's minimum age requirements and is not intended for users below them.

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
